The Smiling Enigma

Jovial, calm, and reserved, Malkinze is the Creator's Left Hand. He is almost always smiling, but beneath that familiar expression lies a character whose loyalty differs from Kasera's. His complete story remains to be uncovered.

👑 His Place in the Creator's Circle

Malkinze serves as the Creator's Left Hand. He shares the Creator's inner circle with Kasera, the Right Hand, whose loyalty is stronger than Malkinze's.
