Island Overview

Discover Skyfall Atoll

Skyfall Atoll is an airborne destination. It is connected to the Divine Realm and information about essence. Special whirlpool gates leading to extra islands are found only within the Divine Realm, so those gates should not be treated as ordinary features scattered across every island.
