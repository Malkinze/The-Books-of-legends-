Official Character Profile

The Creator's Right Hand

Serious, calm, and reserved, Kasera serves as the Creator's Right Hand. His loyalty to the Creator exceeds that of Malkinze, the Left Hand. Behind his composed nature lies a figure whose other forms reveal a different presence.

📜 Character Information

Position The Creator's Right Hand
Personality Serious, calm, reserved
Loyalty Highly loyal to the Creator
Current Profile Normal Form

👑 His Role

Kasera stands as the Creator's Right Hand and is one of the central figures in the Creator's circle.

His loyalty distinguishes him from Malkinze, the Creator's Left Hand. Further details about his duties and history will be added as the official lore is developed.

⚔️ Abilities & Powers

This section is reserved for Kasera's confirmed abilities. His powers will be documented when their official details are ready.

🤝 Relationships

The Creator: Kasera serves as His Majesty's Right Hand and is deeply loyal to him.

Malkinze: The Creator's Left Hand. Malkinze is jovial, calm, reserved, and less loyal to the Creator than Kasera.

🌑 Other Forms

Kasera has additional forms beyond his normal appearance.

His Demon Form is associated with a grim-reaper appearance, a sword, and a scythe. His Angel Form is another established form.

Detailed profiles for these forms will be developed in a future update.
