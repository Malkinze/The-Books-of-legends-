Every legend has a story. Every ruler has a secret. Every realm holds powers beyond imagination.
Enter the archives and discover the beings who shape the Books of Legends universe.

👑
The Creator

His Majesty

The Creator stands at the center of the universe's deepest mysteries. Yet even his understanding has limits. What lies beyond his knowledge remains unknown.

🌀
Beyond the Ordinary

Hallow

An ancient being who existed before the Creator and the realms. Hallow is connected to the path through which the realms came into existence.

⚔️
The Creator's Right Hand

Kasera

Calm, serious, and reserved, Kasera serves as the Creator's right hand. His other forms reveal a far more fearsome presence.

🌑
The Creator's Left Hand

Malkinze

Jovial, calm, and almost always smiling, Malkinze occupies the Creator's left hand. Behind that smile lies a character whose loyalty differs from Kasera's.

📜
A Greater Mystery

Eminence

A significant figure whose appearance in the story begins in Book One. His place in the larger saga reaches beyond a single chapter.

🩸
The Dark Realm

Rhapsody

A false king who took the throne during the Creator's hibernation. His rule, ambitions, and disobedience help set the stage for a greater conflict.

🌌
The Unknown

The Void

The emptiness containing the realms. The Void is part of the greater mystery surrounding the structure and existence of the Books of Legends universe.

🔥
The Final Boss

Great Lord Yami

A mighty enemy whose defeat marks the beginning of the Final Era, an age in which no single person holds the throne.
