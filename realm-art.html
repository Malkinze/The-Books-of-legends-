Realm Art Fundamentals

The current foundations of the Realm Art system. Individual mechanics can vary between arts.

⚔️
Abilities & Cooldowns

Realm Arts provide abilities for combat and other activities. Individual abilities can have their own requirements and cooldowns.

📈
Character Stats

Strength and IQ are the primary stats currently associated with character progression. Exact ability scaling can vary.

🌿
Passive Abilities

Many arts have passive abilities. Regeneration, Endurance, and Observation are examples of established passive names.

🔥
Art Rebirth

Art Rebirth is the established name for the art-evolution system. The exact requirements depend on the art and its finalized design.

🛌
Recovery

Rest is part of the planned recovery system. Food may also be used as an optional recovery mechanic.

🔒
Lost Realm Arts

Lost arts are difficult to regain. Their exact recovery methods remain under development.
