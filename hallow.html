Official Character Profile

The Ancient Mystery

Hallow existed before the Creator and the realms. More than an ordinary being, he stands connected to the ancient path through which the realms came into existence. His knowledge, history, and nature reach into mysteries that few can understand.

📜 Character Information

Nature More than an ordinary being
Origins Existed before the Creator and realms
Personality Funny, calm, reasonable, reserved, cool
Residence Hallow's Tower, Forgotten Palace

🌀 Before the Realms

Hallow's existence predates the Creator and the realms. He is connected to the path used by the Creator to generate the realms.

His full origins and the complete extent of his knowledge remain part of the deeper Books of Legends mythology.

🏰 The Forgotten Palace

Hallow resides within the Forgotten Palace, located inside Hallow's Tower in the Forgotten Kingdom.

Hallow is not confined to his island and can leave it. His presence is tied to a history that reaches beyond the ordinary boundaries of the realms.

👑 Connection to the Creator

Hallow's history reaches back before the Creator and the realms. Their connection is part of the greater mystery surrounding the creation and structure of the universe.

The full details of their relationship will be documented as the official lore develops.

⚔️ Abilities & Power

Hallow possesses extraordinary significance within the Books of Legends universe. His confirmed abilities and limits will be documented when their details are established.

🌌 The Unanswered Questions

What does Hallow know about the Core? What secrets remain hidden in the history of the realms? And how much of his true nature is still unknown?

Some answers belong to the future of Books of Legends.
